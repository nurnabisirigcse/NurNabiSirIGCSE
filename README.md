# NurNabiSirIGCSE

**Cambridge IGCSE video lessons for international students, by Nur Nabi, teaching since 2002.**

YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@NurNabiSirIGCSE

## What is in this repository

Everything I make for my students, in one place: lesson scripts, revision papers, worked solutions, notes and links to my videos.

## Subjects

- **Mathematics 0580**: available now (Paper 2 and Paper 4 revision series)
- **Physics 0625**: coming soon
- **Chemistry 0620**: coming soon
- **Biology 0610**: coming soon

## Folder guide

| Folder | What you will find |
|---|---|
| [0580_Maths/Paper_4](0580_Maths/Paper_4) | Paper 4 revision paper, worked solutions, video scripts |
| [0580_Maths/Paper_2](0580_Maths/Paper_2) | Paper 2 revision paper, worked solutions for videos 1 to 6, video scripts |
| [0580_Maths/Quick_Revision_Decks](0580_Maths/Quick_Revision_Decks) | 10-minute and 15-minute question decks with solutions |
| [Video_Links](Video_Links) | Links to every lesson video on YouTube |

Inside each paper folder: `Revision_Paper` (questions), `Worked_Solutions` (full answers) and `Video_Scripts`.

## How to use it

1. Watch the lesson on YouTube.
2. Pause and attempt each question yourself first.
3. Check your working against the worked solutions.

Based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.
